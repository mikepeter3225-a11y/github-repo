Forgot Your Ascensus Username?

Username Recovery Steps

If you've forgotten your Ascensus username, don't worry. Follow these simple steps to recover it:

  1. On the login page, click "Forgot Username"
  2. Enter your email address associated with your Ascensus account
  3. Verify your identity using our security questions or email verification
  4. Your username will be sent to your email address
  5. Return to the login page and sign in with your recovered username

Need Additional Help?

If you're unable to recover your username through the automated process, our customer service team is available to assist you. Contact Ascensus support for personalized help with your account recovery.
