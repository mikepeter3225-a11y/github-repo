Account Security

Protecting Your Ascensus Account

Ascensus takes the security of your retirement account and personal information very seriously. We employ multiple layers of security to protect your data.

Security Features

  • SSL Encryption: All data transmitted is encrypted using SSL technology
  • Two-Factor Authentication: Add an extra layer of protection to your account
  • Secure Servers: We use secure, monitored servers to store your information
  • Regular Security Audits: We conduct regular security assessments and updates

Best Practices for Your Account

To keep your Ascensus account secure:

  • Never share your username or password
  • Use a strong, unique password
  • Log out after each session, especially on shared computers
  • Be cautious of phishing emails
  • Keep your contact information updated

Reporting Security Issues

If you suspect any unauthorized access to your account or notice any suspicious activity, please contact Ascensus customer support immediately.
