Common Login Issues

Having trouble logging in to your Ascensus account? Here are solutions to common problems:

Can't Remember Your Username?

Use the "Forgot Username" option on the login page to recover your username via email.

Incorrect Password

Ensure caps lock is off and try again. Use "Forgot Password" if you need to reset it.

Account Locked

After multiple failed login attempts, your account may be temporarily locked for security. Contact support to unlock it.

Contact Support

If you need additional assistance, our support team is available to help during business hours.
